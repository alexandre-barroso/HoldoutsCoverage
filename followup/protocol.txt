Fixed-budget verification specification

Use the input, canonical units, targets, fixed predictions and hash definitions
in research/support_protocol.txt. This calculation acquires labels for fixed
predictions; it does not refit models. Cached fitting diagnostics are not fresh
fitting or coefficient-validation evidence.

CONDITIONS AND POLICIES
Use seeds 0 through 199 and three conditions: whitespace/half, casefold/half,
and whitespace/all. The two half conditions share the same selected target S;
all-selection repeats V. Use equal group weights and reject empty targets.
The requested budget is 20 labels. The policies are original eligibility,
separate eligibility, uniform sampling over S, and uniform sampling over the
known LR/NB disagreement set Z. The first three require 20 eligible units;
otherwise record BUDGET_UNATTAINABLE and undefined numerical error. Never top
up, duplicate observations, silently reduce the budget or impute an error.
Disagreement sampling uses k=min(20,len(Z)); empty Z has exact difference zero
without labels, and a fully observed Z has a known finite pair difference.

COUPLED ACQUISITIONS
Order S by representative UTF-8 bytes. Use repetitions 0 through 31. Encode
[separator, 'acquisition', selection_name, seed, repetition] as compact UTF-8
JSON using the exact domain separator in run_followup.permutation. Interpret
the first 16 SHA-256 bytes little-endian as the seed for Generator(PCG64).
Filter one common permutation through each policy's eligible set and take the
first k entries. Normalization is intentionally absent from this key; the two
half conditions share uniform and disagreement acquisitions. All-selection
uses its own selection-name key.

Seal acquisition identities, capacities and prediction-only support counts
before full-label outcome calculations. There are 76,800 acquisition slots
and 2,400 target/policy exact-law slots. These are fixed-design calculations,
not human blinding or new model configurations.

ESTIMATION AND EXACT LAW
The first three policies estimate the acquired mean LR-minus-NB zero-one
loss difference. Disagreement sampling multiplies that mean by len(Z)/len(S).
This scale belongs to the estimator, never to the logical count interval.
Within eligible E let K_minus,K_zero,K_plus count differences -1,0,+1. The
probability of sample counts (a,b,c), a+b+c=k, is
choose(K_minus,a)*choose(K_zero,b)*choose(K_plus,c)/choose(len(E),k).
Use exact integer combination weights and rational arithmetic, enumerating at
most 231 triples for k<=20. Require exact probability-mass conservation.

Compute expected signed, absolute and squared errors, order/tie/wrong-order
probabilities, best/worst pair regret and certificate probabilities. Check the
finite-population mean and variance formulas and independently enumerate small
subsets. Retain the 32 realized acquisitions alongside exact-law expectations.
An infeasible arm has no distribution. Empty and saturated disagreement sets
are explicit special cases. The logical interval is (d +/- D_u)/len(S) under
independently unrestricted missing binary labels, not a sampling confidence
interval or a three-model winner result.

REPORTING AND CHECKS
Retain all capacities and histograms. Paired contrasts use common feasible
targets: separate-original, uniform-original, separate-uniform, and
disagreement-uniform. There are 82 common primary targets for comparisons
with original eligibility; do not compare their mean as a paired effect with
a 200-target mean. Descriptive seed averages are not population inference.

The reference contracts check all scientific record bytes or complete canonical
summaries, while checking execution metadata separately. Corruption fixtures
exercise changed metrics, bool-for-number substitutions, lost records,
malformed manifests and missing bindings. reconstruct.py is a separate
implementation of masks, acquisitions, metrics, capacities and summaries; it
uses sequential SciPy hypergeometric factors for exact-law cross-checks.
readout.py also computes panel diagnostics, which are descriptive explanations
rather than prospective endpoints. Successful execution does not establish
annotation truth, population transport or a universal policy improvement.
